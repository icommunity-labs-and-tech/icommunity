## Privaro

Enterprise AI privacy gateway.

Capabilities:

- PII detection (Presidio + spaCy ES)
- anonymization and pseudonymization
- GDPR and EU AI Act compliance
- immutable blockchain audit evidence via iBS (Polygon network)

Tech stack: FastAPI (Python) · Supabase (PostgreSQL + RLS) · Lovable (React frontend) · iBS API (iCommunity blockchain layer) · Redis

Key architecture: async proxy pattern — requests processed in ~50ms, blockchain certification delivered via webhook. Audit logs are immutable by design.

Status: backend spec complete (v2), MVP in active development.

---

## Revior

AI-powered sales and revenue automation platform.

Co-founded with Mario.

Tagline: *"Escala tus ventas sin aumentar personal."*

Core value proposition: automates the full B2B commercial process for companies that want to grow without a sales team.

Three pillars:

1. **Pipeline generation** — identifies ideal customer profiles, personalizes outreach, runs campaigns with safe limits
2. **Active follow-up** — detects forgotten opportunities, activates automated sequences, prioritizes by close probability
3. **Close and collection acceleration** — automates invoice follow-up, reduces payment delays, minimizes close friction

Platform modules:

- **Ventas B2B** — available now (core module)
- **Ventas B2C** — coming soon
- **Validación de productos** — converts an idea into a real market experiment

Target audience: B2B early-stage founders, SaaS startups, B2B consultancies, SMEs without a sales team.

Tech: React + Vite + TypeScript + Tailwind (Lovable)

Status: early access, pre-revenue.
